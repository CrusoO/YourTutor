# ADR-003: First jurisdiction (D3): India, then Nepal

**Status:** Accepted (founder default)
**Date:** 2026-11-05
**Decision ID:** D3

## Context

The fix plan notes, from research, that the DPDP children's rules apply from about 13 May 2027. Sources differ by one day (13 or 14 May). The EU AI Act education obligations apply from 2 Dec 2027 if Brownie serves the EU.

## Decision

India first. Nepal next, after the April 2027 pilot proves out. Counsel reviews before the pilot.

Brownie never claims legal compliance. It builds what counsel can approve.

## Consequences

- Privacy work is designed around India's DPDP Act: school mode (school as data fiduciary, Brownie as processor), guardian consent records, retention jobs, export and delete.
- Counsel sign-off is a gate for v1.2 (School Ready) and v1.3 (Consent Ready). The founder records it.
- Nepal (Nepali language) is not started before the pilot results.
- Legal points in the plan are labelled engineering views or from-memory items to verify. This ADR makes no legal conclusion.
